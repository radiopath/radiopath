Import pattern

MSI Planet format (.msi, .pln), as shipped by antenna manufacturers. The horizontal and, when present, the vertical pattern are used: 360 attenuation values in dB below the main lobe each. Without a vertical block the elevation cut mirrors the azimuth cut. A gain without a unit is read as dBd.

Or describe one

Builds the usual sector shape: 12 (angle / beamwidth)² dB, capped at the front-to-back ratio. The beamwidth is the 3 dB width, 360 deg gives an omni; the vertical beamwidth shapes the elevation cut the same way. The front-to-back ratio is also the floor: nothing outside the main lobe, in azimuth or elevation, is attenuated more than that. Good enough for planning, not a substitute for the manufacturer's file.
